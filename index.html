Business Disruptions Happen

Every organization faces risk. Whether it is a cyber attack, ransomware incident, severe weather event, utility outage, hardware failure, or human error, disruptions can occur when they are least expected.

The organizations that recover the fastest are often the ones that invested time in planning before the disruption occurred.

Iron Post Continuity helps businesses develop practical plans that improve preparedness, strengthen resilience, and reduce operational impact when unexpected events occur.

How We Help

Business Impact Analysis

Identify critical business functions, dependencies, and recovery priorities.

Business Continuity Planning

Develop strategies that help maintain operations during disruptive events.

Disaster Recovery Planning

Establish recovery procedures for systems, applications, and business data.

Incident Response Planning

Create structured processes for responding to cyber and operational incidents.

Tabletop Exercises

Test plans through realistic scenarios before a real emergency occurs.

Training & Awareness

Help key personnel understand their roles and responsibilities during incidents.

Why Continuity Planning Matters

  • Reduce downtime and operational disruption
  • Improve recovery confidence
  • Protect organizational reputation
  • Support regulatory and contractual requirements
  • Improve communication during emergencies
  • Increase organizational resilience
