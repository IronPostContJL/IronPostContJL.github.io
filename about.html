About Iron Post Continuity

Iron Post Continuity was founded with a simple mission: help organizations prepare for disruptions before they become business-threatening events.

Many small and medium-sized businesses understand the importance of cybersecurity, data protection, and operational resilience, but often lack the time, resources, or expertise to develop comprehensive continuity and recovery plans. Iron Post Continuity exists to bridge that gap.

We work closely with organizations to identify risks, understand operational dependencies, and create practical plans that help maintain business operations during unexpected disruptions.

Meet the Founder

Iron Post Continuity was founded by Josh Little, a United States Marine Corps veteran and information technology professional with experience supporting government, military, and commercial environments.

Throughout his career, Josh has worked with technology systems, end-user support operations, cybersecurity initiatives, and organizational processes that rely on availability, reliability, and effective planning.

His military background instilled the importance of preparation, accountability, and mission readiness—principles that continue to guide Iron Post Continuity's approach to Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery consulting today.

Our Approach

We believe continuity planning should be practical, actionable, and tailored to the unique needs of each organization.

Rather than delivering generic templates, we work directly with clients to understand their operations, identify critical functions, and develop plans that align with their business objectives and risk tolerance.

Our goal is not simply to create documentation. Our goal is to help organizations improve resilience, reduce downtime, and confidently respond to unexpected events.
