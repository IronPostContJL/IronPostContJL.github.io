Who We Are

Iron Post Continuity provides Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery consulting services for small businesses and growing organizations.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: help businesses prepare for disruptions before they become disasters.

Whether it's a cyberattack, equipment failure, severe weather, ransomware incident, power outage, or loss of key personnel, organizations need a documented plan to continue operating and recover efficiently.

We work directly with business owners and leadership teams to create practical, actionable continuity strategies that fit real-world operations.

Why Continuity Matters

  • Protect critical business functions
  • Reduce downtime and financial losses
  • Improve organizational resilience
  • Prepare for cyber incidents and disasters
  • Strengthen customer and stakeholder confidence
  • Support regulatory and contractual requirements
