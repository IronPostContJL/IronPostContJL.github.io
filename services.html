Our Services

Every organization faces risk. Our services are designed to identify vulnerabilities, improve preparedness, and strengthen resilience before disruptions occur.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identify critical business functions, operational dependencies, and recovery priorities.

  • Critical Function Identification
  • Dependency Mapping
  • Operational Impact Assessment
  • Recovery Prioritization
  • RTO & RPO Recommendations

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

Develop documented procedures that help maintain business operations during disruptive events.

  • Continuity Strategies
  • Roles & Responsibilities
  • Communication Plans
  • Emergency Procedures
  • Continuity Documentation

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

Create recovery procedures for systems, applications, infrastructure, and data.

  • Technology Recovery Procedures
  • System Prioritization
  • Backup Validation Review
  • Recovery Sequencing
  • Testing Guidance

Incident Response Planning (IRP)

Establish a structured response process for cybersecurity incidents and operational emergencies.

  • Incident Classification
  • Response Procedures
  • Escalation Paths
  • Communication Workflows
  • Response Checklists

Recovery Objectives (RTO / RPO)

Define acceptable downtime and data loss thresholds to support continuity planning.

  • Downtime Analysis
  • Data Loss Analysis
  • Business Requirements Review
  • Recovery Target Development

Tabletop Exercises

Validate plans through realistic scenarios before an actual disruption occurs.

  • Scenario Development
  • Facilitated Exercise
  • Gap Identification
  • After Action Reporting

Our Engagement Process

Every project follows a structured methodology designed to deliver practical and actionable results.

1. Discover

Learn about your organization, operations, objectives, and current preparedness efforts.

2. Assess

Identify risks, dependencies, vulnerabilities, and continuity gaps.

3. Plan

Develop continuity and recovery documentation tailored to your organization.

4. Test

Validate plans through reviews, walkthroughs, and tabletop exercises.

5. Improve

Refine plans as business operations, technology, and organizational needs evolve.
