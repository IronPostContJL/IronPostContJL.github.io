Our Services

Iron Post Continuity helps organizations prepare for, respond to, and recover from operational disruptions. Our services are designed to strengthen organizational resilience, reduce downtime, and improve confidence during unexpected events.

Whether your organization is just beginning its continuity journey or looking to improve existing plans, we provide practical guidance tailored to your business.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Before developing recovery strategies, organizations must understand which processes are most critical to their success. A Business Impact Analysis identifies essential functions, dependencies, and the consequences of downtime.

Deliverables

  • Critical business function identification
  • Operational dependency mapping
  • Impact assessment
  • Recovery prioritization
  • Recovery objective recommendations

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

A Business Continuity Plan provides documented procedures that help organizations continue operating during disruptive events.

The objective is to maintain critical business functions while minimizing operational and financial impacts.

Deliverables

  • Continuity strategies
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Communication procedures
  • Emergency response guidance
  • Business continuity documentation

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

Disaster Recovery Planning focuses on restoring systems, applications, infrastructure, and data after an outage or disaster.

These plans provide structured recovery procedures designed to reduce downtime and accelerate restoration efforts.

Deliverables

  • Technology recovery procedures
  • System recovery prioritization
  • Backup validation recommendations
  • Recovery sequencing documentation
  • Recovery testing guidance

Incident Response Planning (IRP)

Cybersecurity incidents require a coordinated response. An Incident Response Plan establishes the procedures, responsibilities, and communication processes necessary to manage security events.

Deliverables

  • Incident classification framework
  • Response procedures
  • Escalation paths
  • Communication workflows
  • Incident response checklists

Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) & Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)

Recovery objectives help organizations determine acceptable levels of downtime and data loss. These objectives serve as the foundation for continuity and recovery planning efforts.

Deliverables

  • Business requirement discussions
  • Downtime tolerance analysis
  • Data loss tolerance analysis
  • Recovery objective recommendations

Tabletop Exercises

A plan that has never been tested may not perform as expected during a real-world incident. Tabletop exercises provide a safe environment for teams to practice decision-making and identify areas for improvement.

Exercise Benefits

  • Plan validation
  • Gap identification
  • Role clarification
  • Improved communication
  • Increased organizational readiness

Training & Awareness

Effective plans require informed personnel. Training and awareness sessions help ensure employees understand their responsibilities before a disruption occurs.

Topics May Include

  • Business continuity fundamentals
  • Disaster recovery procedures
  • Incident response responsibilities
  • Emergency communications
  • Preparedness best practices
